**Added** — The viewer opens an annotations sidecar with **Open an annotations file** once a transcript shows, and hands it to `<telelux-transcript>` to render and review. Invalid JSON and schema mismatches are reported with their reasons, and the current annotations stay. A new `<script type="application/json" id="annotations">` slot bakes a sidecar into a page alongside its baked transcript; a `#v=1&data=` fragment ignores it.
